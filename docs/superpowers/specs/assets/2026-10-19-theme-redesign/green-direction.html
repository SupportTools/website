Keeping your green and your logo

Your bean's lime green (#60a818) only reaches 2.95:1 on white, so it can't be used for body text or links. In each option below, lime is the brand color for bars, borders, chips, and buttons. Text and links use darker greens from the same family (5–10:1). Light mode is on the left, dark on the right.
